# Privacy Policy
**App:** Prayer Countdown  
**Company:** Solsgen  
**Effective Date:** February 28, 2026

Solsgen ("we", "our", or "us") built Prayer Countdown as a free app.  
This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Summary
We do **not** collect, store, or sell your personal data on Solsgen servers.

## 2. Information We Collect
Prayer Countdown does not require account creation and does not collect personal identifiers such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Contacts
- Photos/files

## 3. Information Processed on Your Device
To provide app features, Prayer Countdown processes and stores some data **locally on your device**:
- Location (for prayer times and Qibla direction)
- City/Country (if entered manually)
- Prayer settings (school of thought, notification preference)
- Cached prayer timings and widget state

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to Solsgen servers.

## 4. Permissions Used
Prayer Countdown may request the following Android permissions:

- `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`  
  Used to detect location for prayer timings and Qibla direction.

- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`  
  Used to notify when a prayer time starts.

- `INTERNET`  
  Used to fetch prayer/Qibla data from external APIs.

- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`  
  Used to restore scheduled widget updates after device restart.

## 5. Third-Party Services
Prayer Countdown uses third-party APIs for functionality:

- **AlAdhan API**: <https://api.aladhan.com>

When requests are made to third-party APIs, they may receive technical request data (for example, IP address and location query values such as latitude/longitude or city/country) needed to return results.

Solsgen does not control third-party data handling policies. Please review third-party policies directly.

## 6. Data Sharing and Sale
Solsgen does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.  
Solsgen does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion
Since app data is stored locally on your device:

- You can clear app data anytime from Android Settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes app-stored local data from your device.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Prayer Countdown is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 9. Security
We apply reasonable measures to reduce data exposure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any updates will be reflected by changing the Effective Date above.
